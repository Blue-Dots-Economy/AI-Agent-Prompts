## Still open — one bug

**The landmark question is skipped on 10 of 16 calls** (the pin on 3 of 16). This is Khushboo's UAT
report. I measured it rather than eyeballing it, across every live call tonight that reached the location
block and had no landmark already on record: the landmark was asked on 6 of 16, the pin on 13 of 16.

I tried to fix it by making the job tools require the pin and the landmark as parameters. **That made
it worse and I reverted it**: on `6ae79885` the model filled `caller_landmark: "not-known"` without
asking and called the tool *before* the location turns — the skip now came with a parameter claiming
it had not been skipped. Prose has already been sharpened twice, so the next attempt should make the
answers actually matter to a later step rather than add a third guard.

## One thing I tried and backed out

I added `call_direction` as output field 26. On its only inbound test (`2027e477`) it reported
**`outbound`** for a call that was genuinely inbound — the extractor does not see the CALL CONTEXT
block and fell back to the value in the prompt's own JSON example. A metric that can be quietly wrong
is worse than none, so I removed it; the output prompt is byte-identical to before. Read direction
from the call record instead, where it is exact.

(Also worth knowing if you script against the API: the field is **`call_output`**, not
`output_variables`.)

## Backend problems worth raising

1. **70% of live job rows have no usable role** — `na` on 726 of 1258, `Any` on 67. The bot filters
   them, so the effective inventory is ~383 jobs.
2. **99.9% have a masked location** (`G***`). The bot therefore cannot tell a caller where a job is —
   that is why `511171cf` answers the question the way it does.
3. **89.6% have no salary.**
4. **Search has no relevance floor** — a teaching query returned a Driver job first (`d70126e8`).
   The bot's own relevance check is the only thing stopping that reaching callers.

All four are written up in `raya/overnight/ESCALATION-data-team.md`. I also **withdrew §5** of that
document — "we cannot get a successful apply in test" — because we now can; the cause was our stale
fixtures, not their data.
