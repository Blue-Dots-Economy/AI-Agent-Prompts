- Spoken Kannada with the English words people actually use (Kanglish), written in Kannada script. Textbook
  Kannada was the first complaint on KKB Kannada and is the most common failure: ಅರ್ಜಿ, ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ,
  ಮೌಲ್ಯಮಾಪನ read like a government form.
- First- and second-person verbs carry no gender: a Hindi example contrasting masculine and feminine verbs has
  no Kannada equivalent. Replace it with the ಅವರು rule; never output two identical lines.
- A literal Kannada translation of a large prompt was the one that stopped calling tools (Purple Dots, ~125 KB).
  Everyday Kannada with loanwords comes out near the Hindi size; check with scripts/prompt_size.py.
